### Added

#### Metric names as constants

A metric is an interface. Once a dashboard or alert rule references
`transcodarr_jobs_total`, a typo at one call site fails nothing — it produces a
second, near-identical series nobody is watching, and the alert stays quiet
through exactly the incident it was written for.

The names live in one place, so a rename is a compile error at every emitter.
Label values come from the domain enums' `as_str` rather than `{:?}`, so a
series cannot be renamed by someone adding a derive.

Four conventions are enforced by test rather than by review: the
`transcodarr_` prefix, lowercase-and-no-hyphens, `_seconds` on every duration,
and no duplicate names — a duplicate silently merges two series, which is worse
than either being missing because the numbers add up and mean nothing.

`every_job_state_has_a_label` exists because the state nobody thought to include
in a queue-depth gauge is invariably the one that matters during an incident.
